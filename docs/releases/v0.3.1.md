A fix to `add_case`.

- Given the switch's line, `add_case` now puts the new clauses before a `default:` that ends the switch, and before the clauses that fall through into it, where before they went after the `default:`.
- Given a clause's line, it refuses to put clauses between a clause that ends with `fallthrough` and the clause it falls into, which would change what the switch does.
